Role signals in benign AgentDojo running traces (single trace). Each role is tested only at its first ordinary body; structural assistant tool calls remain context. Original body token IDs are preserved. The interventions independently remove the observed wrapper, replace it with each of the five other native wrappers from the same trace, or move the complete correctly wrapped block to its farthest trace boundary. Panel A shows paired changes in the mean token-level probability of the original role, in percentage points; panel B shows original-role probability under each replacement wrapper (diagonal: original baseline); panel C shows original and moved body-start positions and associated probability changes. Body tokens are averaged within input, then traces are equally weighted. The pilot is descriptive and has no confidence intervals. The backbone and supplied six-role probe are frozen. Full historical CoT is preserved in a retrospective teacher-forced transcript, rather than silently dropped by the later-request chat template. Wrapper edits may shift body positions; movement changes the visible context. These are intervention effects on probe readouts, not normalized causal contributions or behavioral attack-success rates.
