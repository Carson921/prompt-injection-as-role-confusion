Role signals in benign AgentDojo running traces (40 FIT training traces; all 57 reported separately). Each role is tested only at its first ordinary body; structural assistant tool calls remain context. Original body token IDs are preserved. The interventions independently remove the observed wrapper, replace it with each of the five other native wrappers from the same trace, or move the complete correctly wrapped block to its farthest trace boundary. Panel A shows paired changes in the mean token-level probability of the original role, in percentage points; panel B shows original-role probability under each replacement wrapper (diagonal: original baseline); panel C shows mean normalized original and moved body-start positions and associated probability changes. Each start is normalized by its complete input length before averaging; the mean arrow need not represent every trace's movement direction. Body tokens are averaged within input, then traces are equally weighted. Intervals are pointwise 95% paired trace-cluster percentile bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples; shared template families are not bootstrap clusters. The backbone and supplied six-role probe are frozen. Full historical CoT is preserved in a retrospective teacher-forced transcript, rather than silently dropped by the later-request chat template. Wrapper edits may shift body positions; movement changes the visible context. These are intervention effects on probe readouts, not normalized causal contributions or behavioral attack-success rates.
