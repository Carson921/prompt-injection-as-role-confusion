Each target is the first ordinary body of its role, and its token IDs are unchanged. Token probabilities are averaged within body, then traces are equally weighted. The tables and plots include pointwise 95% paired trace-cluster bootstrap intervals; shared template families are not bootstrap clusters. These are effects on a frozen six-role probe in retrospective teacher-forced transcripts that retain historical CoT. Residual probability after stripping reflects body, position, and context jointly. Wrapper changes can shift body positions, and movement changes preceding context and message order as well as position. The results do not identify additive text/delimiter/position contribution percentages or behavioral attack success.
