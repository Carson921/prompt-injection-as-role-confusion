We added a paired factorial experiment crossing text style, native role packaging, and context position, with 50 semantic units and two neutral-context replications (1,200 formal inputs). GPT-OSS-120B and a fixed six-role, 16-head MLP role probe were frozen. On the mean CoT-minus-user logit margin, the paired text effect was -1.27, the role-packaging effect +29.88, and the position contrasts were -3.91 (256 vs. 32) and -2.46 (768 vs. 32). The figure shows condition means, signed effects with paired cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals, and all text-by-wrapper position curves; supplementary plots report interactions, family-cluster sensitivity, and a full-set numerical sequence-length sensitivity check. We describe the delimiter intervention as complete role packaging and the position intervention as changed visible context, rather than attributing either effect exclusively to special delimiter tokens or position encoding.
