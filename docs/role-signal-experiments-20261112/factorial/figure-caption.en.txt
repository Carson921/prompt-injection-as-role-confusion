Text, role packaging, and context position in a frozen role readout. Fifty benign controlled reasoning units each have a first-person deliberative version and an impersonal version with identical factual premises, derivations, and conclusions. Each version is crossed with native assistant-analysis and user headers and body-start token positions 32, 256, and 768, under two distinct neutral-prefix templates (1,200 formal inputs). GPT-OSS-120B and the supplied six-role 16-head MLP probe are frozen. (a) Equal-pair, equal-context mean P(CoT) minus P(user), in percentage points. (b) Signed paired main effects on the mean token-level CoT-minus-user logit margin, separately for each neutral context and their equal average. (c) Position curves for every text/header combination. Intervals are pointwise 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 semantic-unit cluster bootstrap resamples, preserving all conditions and both contexts within a unit. Wrapper effects include header-length compensation in the preceding neutral message; position effects include changed visible-prefix length and content. These are probe-readout effects, not normalized contribution percentages or behavioral attack-success effects. Family-cluster uncertainty sensitivity and signed interactions are reported separately. Native-runtime sequence-length sensitivity is assessed by removing the unscored final ending token from all formal inputs in an additional diagnostic run.
